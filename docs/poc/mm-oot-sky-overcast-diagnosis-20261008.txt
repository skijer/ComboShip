ComboShip / 2Ship OoT sky overcast diagnosis — 2026-10-08

Finding
The original OoT sky port interprets MM's native CLOUD texture choice as OoT overcast artwork. In the supplied native MM archive, Termina Field and the four checked Clock Town scene headers use initial skybox configuration 10. Environment_Init resolves that to configuration 25 on First Day. That schedule uses the CLOUD texture throughout 06:00–16:00 and the FINE texture after the 16:00–17:00 transition. Thus noon maps to the Reloaded gray overcast artwork, while 23:00 maps to its clear night artwork.

MM's CLOUD resource describes cloud geometry, not exclusively storm weather. Its normal First Day noon palette in this schedule has primitive RGB 235/250/235 and environment RGB 0/4/199. The native renderer applies this blue coloring. The port deliberately uses neutral coloring for the already-colored OoT artwork, so mapping ordinary MM daytime CLOUD to gray OoT overcast changes the visual meaning.

The MM Audio Editor 'Overcast During Outdoor Rain' checkbox controls only the additional MM rain layer. Disabling it returns the native schedule, which can still select CLOUD. The OoT and MM Audio Editor settings are separate; cor confirmed changing the MM controls. The Rod of Seasons is not the origin: cor reports this behavior predates its integration, and the diagnostic reproduces it with the season Off.

Rod of Seasons sky policy — requested by cor

Spring: Soft overcast while its rain is active. Cor delegated this choice;
the chosen direction is a gentle, rain-consistent sky rather than a clear sky
during persistent rain. Keep the established Spring rain schedule.

Summer: Clear skies on all three days. Use the clear OoT dawn/day/sunset/night
artwork as appropriate for the clock; retain stars and normal time transitions.

Autumn: Clear skies between showers. Fade to overcast as intermittent rain
arrives, then fade back to clear as the rain ends. Cloud cover follows the rain.

Winter: Overcast skies throughout the active season.

These are the requested Rod presentation targets, not claims of implemented
behavior. Preserve the current separate Audio Editor controls and their explicit
global-weather override priority, plus existing story/cutscene/interior boundaries.

Current implementation versus the targets
- Spring already adds its own overcast while Spring rain is active; that layer
  bypasses the Audio Editor rain-overcast checkbox.
- Summer forces the rendered sky clear only on Day 2. Days 1 and 3 still inherit
  native cloud selection, which can show the gray OoT overcast sky.
- Autumn already has intermittent rain and an associated overcast fade, but
  a native CLOUD baseline stays cloudy during dry intervals. The composition
  adds cloud cover; it cannot remove native CLOUD to reach the requested clear sky.
- Winter already contributes a 0.65 overcast layer independent of the Audio
  Editor checkbox; the final appearance also depends on the native baseline.

Scope and merge status
Diagnosis and seasonal target notes only. No game source, sacred scene archives,
Reloaded archive, or integration branches were changed; no patch was pushed or
merged. Correcting the original daytime mapping and implementing the recorded
Summer/Autumn targets remain future game-code work. They require distinguishing
ordinary MM daytime clouds from native/enhanced storms and story gloom, then
composing the active season's clear/overcast policy instead of treating CLOUD
alone as a storm signal.
