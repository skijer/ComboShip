# Door-only cross-game entrance options

Keep the existing Cross-Game Interiors checkbox and its complete legacy pool.
Add independent Cross-Game Shops and Cross-Game Door Interiors checkboxes, off
by default. Both narrow choices require an actual door-opening transition;
Kokiri Shop is an example of the excluded open-passage category, not an exception.
Shops exchange with shops; other door interiors exchange with other door interiors.
The broad option takes precedence and the menu disables the redundant narrow
choices without erasing their stored preferences. Native entrance settings remain
independent, and these controls apply when generating a new seed.

Classify physical ingress, not whole scenes. Preserve the original Mask Shop /
Clock Tower portal exclusions. Retail purchase interiors count as shops, including
Granny's potion building and MM's Milk Bar; games, services and Curiosity Shop's
back hideout belong to other interiors. Open shops, Kokiri houses, caves, fairy
fountains, tents, the Temple of Time and the Observatory's Clock Town passage
remain native under the narrow choices. MM's Zora Hall room doors are real doors.
Keep source access rules, opening schedules, paired returns and recovery intact.

Persist narrow pools separately, support v1/v2 replay unchanged, and reject
mixed-category permutations and malformed options. Existing saves and recorded
maps must not be regenerated or migrated. No scene pack, save format or visual
asset changes are needed for this additional setting.

Integrate the previously approved forest/transition candidate and all existing
PR43 generation, Goron, Somaria and equipment corrections into PR43's existing
base after relevant checks. User explicitly authorized publication and merge on
2026-10-16. Preview approval is visual approval, not installed-game proof.
